# Sponsor & Outreach V1: review contract

Status: draft implementation for review in Team Hub PR #6. The migration has not been applied to production, and the conservative access policy below requires explicit approval before any production rollout. No real contact import, scraping, communication, Finance mutation, or deployment is included.

## Boundary and authorization

Outreach records relationships and promises. Finance remains authoritative for receipts, expected income, amounts, spending, and approvals. An outreach pledge is never a receipt; a fulfilled logo placement or thank-you does not alter any financial status. There is no send button, notification job, payment path, receipt creation, or approval API.

The proposed V1 access policy permits only profiles that are currently active with the live base role `admin` or `mentor`. Student/lead roles, team positions (including business leadership), and client/JWT metadata do not confer Outreach access. Engagement and recognition owners are restricted to those same eligible current managers. This proposal must remain undeployed until the production access decision is approved.

The private schema contains organizations, contacts, engagements, conversations, pledges, recognition, income links, immutable audit history, and operation receipts. Every table enables RLS with no client policies, and direct table/sequence access is revoked from PUBLIC, anon, authenticated, and service_role, including inherited default grants. Only authenticated callers receive schema usage plus five guarded private entry points, reached through five public SECURITY INVOKER wrappers. The private SECURITY DEFINER entry points use an empty search_path and explicit identity/live-role checks. The definer is needed to reach RPC-only storage; no existing Finance or identity grants change. No client receives EXECUTE on the internal guard or trigger helpers.

## Model

- Organization: a persistent organization or individual sponsor identity, name, website, internal notes, active/archive flag.
- Business contact: a distinct person affiliated with one organization, with title, work contact details, notes, and active/archive flag. Contacts do not become Hub team users. V1 is for business contacts, not student/minor contact records.
- Engagement: exactly one organization/Finance season relationship, with stage, one eligible owner, next follow-up date, and notes. Stages are prospect, contacted, discussing, committed, closed. Closing an engagement describes the relationship and has no effect on Finance.
- Conversation: an append-only dated email/phone/meeting/other summary tied to an engagement and optional contact from the same organization. Corrections are new notes. Recording an email or call never sends one.
- Pledge: a cash promise with a positive decimal amount or an in-kind promise with no amount, description, promised/expected dates, and pledged/canceled status. No `received` or payment-completion field exists.
- Recognition: a logo placement, recognition item, thank-you, or other promised obligation, independently assigned with a due date. Status is promised/fulfilled/waived. Fulfilled requires a fulfillment date; other statuses require no fulfillment date. A fulfillment note can identify evidence or explain the result. An optional pledge must belong to the same engagement.
- Income link: a manual association of existing same-season Finance income with an active cash pledge. One income record links to at most one pledge; a pledge may have multiple income references. Values and status remain in Finance and are read fresh. V1 has no unlink/reassignment endpoint; a mistaken association requires a separately reviewed correction. No totals are copied or synthesized from links.

Organization/season identity on existing engagements, organization identity on contacts, and engagement identity on existing pledges/recognition cannot be moved. Archived organizations and closed Finance seasons reject new or edited dependent records. Archiving preserves history and links. Existing records may reference an owner who later loses eligibility; further edits require choosing an eligible owner or clearing that owner.

## RPC contract

Types are in `src/outreach/types.ts`.

`outreach_context(selected_season uuid = null)` returns contract_version 1, the live user_id, can_manage=true, can_link_finance, season_id, seasons, owners, organizations, contacts, engagements, conversations, pledges, recognition, income, income_links. Ineligible callers receive SQLSTATE 42501 and no data. An explicit unknown season receives 22023. A null season selects active first, otherwise most recently created; with no Finance seasons it returns season_id=null and empty seasonal arrays. Season summaries contain only identity, name, status, and dates, never starting funds/reserves. Organizations and contacts are global; other records are scoped to the selected season. Context currently returns the complete small-team dataset without silent truncation; pagination and searchable audit history are not V1 features. Growth beyond a small team should be evaluated before expanding this contract.

`outreach_save(entity text, p jsonb, request_id uuid, expected_actor uuid)` creates/edits one organization/contact/engagement/conversation/pledge/recognition. Create with a client-generated record UUID and version 0; update with the current version and complete editable fields. The server owns author/time/version. Returns `{id, version}`. A mismatched expected_actor fails 42501. A missing/null/negative version fails 22023; a stale update or nonzero create version fails 40001; malformed/unsupported relationships fail 22023; relational/check constraints also protect values. Data is not partially updated on failure.

`outreach_link_income(pledge_id uuid, income_id uuid, request_id uuid, expected_actor uuid)` returns `{id, version:1}` for an existing or newly attached link. Both Outreach authorization and the unchanged `finance_private.can_manage_budget()` must permit the actor. It only reads/locks the existing Finance income row. Linking a different pledge to already-associated income is rejected. Source text is not parsed to infer sponsors; selecting a same-season income reference is an explicit manager action, and the amounts need not equal a pledge (for example, installments).

`outreach_request_status(request_id uuid, expected_actor uuid)` returns `{status:'applied', result:{id,version}}`, `{status:'canceled'}`, or `{status:'not_found'}` for that actor only. It checks live Outreach access; Finance-link receipts additionally check current Finance budget access. It reveals no input payload or another actor's operation. Not found is not proof that an in-flight request has failed; keep an uncertain outcome distinct from an explicit rejection, refresh, and reconcile before offering another write.

`outreach_cancel_request(request_id uuid, expected_actor uuid)` returns `{status:'applied', result:{id,version}}` when the original write already committed, otherwise `{status:'canceled'}` after inserting or observing an immutable cancellation tombstone. This is the fifth RPC, included in the proposed production-access policy. It takes the same transaction lock as save/link and rechecks live Outreach access after waiting. Already-applied Finance-link results still require current Finance budget access. An absent request can be canceled without guessing what it contained; the tombstone stores only the actor/request identity, timestamp, empty payload, and canceled outcome. It never deletes, edits, or undoes a business record, income link, or audit entry. Repeated cancellation returns the same outcome without another receipt. A late original save/link sees the tombstone and fails SQLSTATE 22023 (`Request was canceled before application`). If the original write wins the lock first, cancellation returns its applied result without undoing it.

The recovery UI uses the original pending UUID, not a new operation or a persisted contact payload. It clears the pending receipt only after validating canceled or the matching original applied outcome. Cancellation timeout, denial, and malformed response keep the receipt pending; a later status check or cancellation can resolve it. The explicit cancellation action is appropriate when a status check still returns not_found. Its outcome is final only once the canceled/applied response is verified.

Each operation UUID belongs to one actor and exact operation/payload. Exact replay returns the original result and does not duplicate the mutation, update its timestamp, or append another audit event. Reusing the token with different input is rejected. Retrying after another edit still returns the first operation result. Live authorization is checked before replay. The small-team V1 serializes mutations with a short transaction-scoped advisory lock, then checks row versions and validates/locks relationships. Authority is rechecked after lock waits, including Finance authority after the income-row lock. Audit insert and immutable operation receipt commit in the same transaction as the mutation. Audit failure rolls back the entire write.

Income metadata and links are returned only when the existing Finance budget guard allows them, even if Finance policy later changes. The exposed read-only subset is id, season_id, source, income_type, amount, status, expected_on, received_on, reference. Finance notes, category data, receipt totals, and permissions are not copied into Outreach.

## Dependencies and source

Migration: `supabase/migrations/20261006045839_sponsor_outreach_v1.sql`, created by official Supabase CLI 2.119.0 using `migration new sponsor_outreach_v1`. Prerequisite is the existing Finance budget core, including finance_seasons, finance_income, and finance_private.can_manage_budget. The migration fails if that dependency is absent; it does not create a substitute Finance model. Finance seasons are reused as-is rather than duplicating their lifecycle. No existing public table or function is replaced, and the Finance checkout was inspected read-only.

`tests/fixtures/outreach_finance_budget_core.sql` is an unchanged copy of Finance main d8fd913's `202609200001_finance_budget_core.sql`; synthetic tests pair it with the Hub's existing Finance V1 fixture. Fixture values use synthetic names and example.test contacts. All tests are disposable and do not read production data.

## Verification and remaining gates

- `tests/outreach-db.spec.ts`: 14 PGlite integration groups covering live access, distinct contact/organization/season relationships, actor binding, replay/mismatch/stale versions, immutable conversation entries, pledge/recognition constraints, Finance-only linking/read-through, guarded recovery after revocation, closed/archive rules, direct privilege denial, audit rollback, and cancellation tombstone identity/idempotency/no-undo guards. Snapshots verify the migration and Outreach operations preserve Finance records, grants, RLS flags, and the exact Finance budget guard definition.
- `tests/native/outreach-concurrency.mjs`: eight reproducible PostgreSQL multi-session cases for duplicate replay, competing versioned edits, actor revocation while awaiting the global lock or record lock, Finance revocation while awaiting the income row, cancellation versus in-flight/late writes, and actor revocation during the cancellation lock wait. Run with `OUTREACH_PG_BIN=/path/to/postgresql/bin node tests/native/outreach-concurrency.mjs`. All eight cases passed on native PostgreSQL 17.6. This executor could not create Unix sockets, so the runner uses the existing bridge test pattern: an ephemeral port bound only to 127.0.0.1, Unix sockets disabled, a disposable synthetic database, and no production connection settings. It stops and removes the temp cluster on exit. No externally reachable listener is created.
- Production-specific database advisors remain a release gate. No remote advisors or production queries were run because the current scope is staged implementation and draft-PR validation only.
- Production access approval, actual installed Finance dependency review, backup/rollout review, and user-facing validation are outside the approved draft-PR implementation and must precede any deployment.
