Team 4418 Privacy Notice

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Please review this Privacy Notice before using Team 4418’s tools. It explains how we collect, use, and protect information. This notice does not replace any consent required by law.

1. Who we are and what this notice covers

Team 4418 operates the team’s online tools, including Team Hub, Inventory, Pit Operations and Finance. This notice explains how these tools handle information about students, mentors, volunteers and other people involved with the team. It also covers any public event pages published through Team Hub. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to First4418@gmail.com.

The tools support team administration and robotics activities. Different features are available to different accounts. A link to another website does not make that website part of these tools; its own privacy practices apply.

2. Information we collect

  • Accounts and membership: names, email addresses, account identifiers, team roles, functional areas, membership status, leadership positions and invitation records. Supabase handles sign-in and password-related account functions.
  • Attendance and participation: meeting rosters, check-in and departure times, STEM outreach service hours, attendance status, absence or schedule-change requests, explanations, review decisions, strikes and records of corrections or follow-up actions.
  • Team activities: task assignments, comments, project and engineering records, uploaded drawings and photos, inventory changes, competition assignments, scouting observations and notes, and the person and time associated with an action.
  • Volunteer, finance and outreach records: volunteer activities and hours, purchase requests and approvals, expenses and income records, and sponsor or organizational contact details. These features can include notes and supporting references.
  • Communications and technical information: announcements, notification recipients and delivery status, and records needed to operate accounts and services. Hosting and service providers receive connection information, such as IP addresses and browser request information, when a device uses their services.

Information may come from you, authorized team members, team administrators or connected competition-data services. Notes, images and files can contain personal information. Include only what is needed. Avoid putting medical details, family circumstances, passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information in attendance explanations, robot notes or uploads. Ask a team mentor how to handle a sensitive situation privately.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide accounts and role-based access, coordinate attendance and team work, manage equipment and competition activities, record volunteer hours, administer team finances and outreach, and deliver team communications. Activity histories support corrections, accountability, troubleshooting and security. Attendance information also supports human review under the team’s attendance policy. STEM outreach totals support human varsity-letter review for each May 1–April 30 year; previous years’ records remain available.

4. Who can see information

Access depends on a person’s active account, role, assigned responsibilities and the feature being used. The sites are not a private diary: information entered for team operations may be visible to other authorized team members.

  • Shared account profiles, including display names and email addresses, are accessible to active authorized suite accounts.
  • Students can view their own attendance records and STEM outreach totals. Authorized leadership can access broader attendance, STEM outreach totals, verified-time corrections and review information. STEM outreach event details appear on the signed-in TeamHub attendance calendar; personal totals are not published on public event pages. Permission to view records and permission to make decisions are separate.
  • Operational records, including inventory and Pit/scouting information, are shared with authorized team accounts. Scouting reports can identify the submitting scout to signed-in team members.
  • Finance and outreach access depends on the relevant permissions. Announcements may be limited to selected team audiences. Authorized administrators can have broader management access.
  • Mentor volunteer entries and correction histories are restricted to the account holder. Authorized team reporting provides aggregate volunteer information, without exposing another volunteer’s private entry notes or correction history through that reporting feature.

Technical administrators and service providers may have access needed to operate, support or secure the systems. Authorized users may also download permitted reports or files. A downloaded copy is separate from the live site and may remain on the recipient’s device.

5. Services used by the tools

The suite uses the following services for its features:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database storage, access controls, file storage and server-side functions.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the website application files. Google Fonts: providing fonts used by the sites; loading those fonts connects your browser to Google’s services.
  • Resend: delivering team notification emails. Email delivery requires recipient addresses and the message content. Account invitations and recovery messages use the configured Supabase authentication email system.
  • The Blue Alliance, Nexus and Statbotics: competition schedules, event information and public team statistics. These integrations request public competition information; the Pit integration does not send private scouting observations to Statbotics.
  • YouTube and Twitch: optional event video. Opening a stream connects your browser to the selected provider, whose own privacy and storage practices apply.

External links, including team resources and document links, may take you to services with their own accounts and privacy notices. This notice does not describe every practice of those services.

6. Public pages and uploaded content

The public launcher and any published public event guides can be viewed without a team account. Protected operational records are separate from public event content. Information deliberately included in a public page may be copied or shared by others. Do not put student rosters, attendance details, disciplinary records, medical information or unapproved personal contact details in public content.

Inventory images, announcement images and fabrication files use protected storage and feature-specific access controls. A photo or document may still reveal people or personal details within the content. Only upload material appropriate for its authorized team audience.

7. Browser storage and shared devices

The suite uses browser storage to support sign-in and application functions. Team Hub maintains the shared sign-in session for the connected suite. Some features also keep temporary operation references needed to recover an interrupted action.

Pit scouting saves drafts and submitted-report queues in browser localStorage. Drafts are not sent to the team database until submitted. Submitted reports can upload automatically when the same signed-in account and event reconnect. Signing out hides these local records in the app but does not erase them. This device storage is not encrypted by the scouting application.

Use a trusted device and sign out when finished. On a shared device, clearing the relevant site data removes local copies but can also remove unsent work and sign-in information. Confirm that reports are synced before clearing storage. Exported backups and downloaded reports need separate handling. Optional external video providers may use their own cookies or other storage.

8. How long information is kept

The current tools retain account and operational records on an ongoing basis, including historical activity and audit records. Disabling an account stops access but does not automatically delete its information. Corrections, rescinded strikes, voided volunteer entries and superseded scouting reports may remain in history. Resetting a displayed annual total does not erase the underlying records.

There is no single automatic deletion period across the suite. Requests to remove or anonymize information are reviewed in light of the record’s purpose, its links to other team records, and any applicable school or legal retention obligations. Removal from the active system may not immediately remove copies in provider backups, delivered emails or previously downloaded files. Local scouting drafts, backups and downloads can remain on a device until separately removed.

9. Questions, access and correction requests

Contact First4418@gmail.com to ask what information is held about you, request a correction, ask about closing an account, or request deletion or anonymization. Parents and guardians may contact the team about a student’s information. Identify the relevant account and describe the request, but do not email passwords or unnecessary sensitive details.

The team may need to verify identity and authority before providing records or making a change. Requests are reviewed individually; some corrections are recorded through an audit entry rather than by removing the original record. Where information must be retained, account deactivation or limits on further use may be appropriate. Applicable legal rights and school requirements are not replaced by this notice.

10. Security and changes to this notice

The tools use account authentication, role-based permissions and database access controls to restrict protected records. No online service or device can be guaranteed secure. Report a suspected privacy or account-security issue to First4418@gmail.com without including a password or private access link.

This notice may be updated as the tools and their information practices change. The published notice will show its effective or updated date. Questions about a change can be sent to the contact above.
